The loop explained

How it works.

Seven steps. One continuous cycle.

01
YouTube creates content and builds attention. Content

Gaming videos attract an audience organically. Every video is an entry point into the wider ecosystem.

02
Social media distributes that attention. Social

TikTok, Twitter/X and Facebook extend the reach beyond YouTube and introduce new people to the brand.

03
Social profiles point to Linktree. Social

Every social bio links to the relevant Linktree, ensuring no visitor reaches a dead end.

04
Linktree provides access to the full ecosystem. Loop

Two Linktrees — one for the content side, one for the store — centralise all destinations and keep traffic moving within the ecosystem.

05
Shopify converts attention into customers. Commerce

An audience already engaged with the content brand is more likely to trust and buy from the store.

06
The store's social channels reconnect customers with the content ecosystem. Commerce

Instagram, TikTok and Facebook for the store also link back to YouTube, closing the loop and deepening the relationship.

07
The cycle repeats. Loop

Every new piece of content, every social post, every sale reinforces all other parts of the ecosystem. One business. Two engines. No dead ends.
